Task 1: Transactions by day of the week

How many transactions were registered on Fridays? 6,553 transactions were registered on Fridays between 1 January 2015 and 31 August 2026. Friday is low mainly because it was part of the UAE weekend until January 2022: there were only 299 Friday transactions in 2015–2021, then 740 in 2022, rising to about 1,750 a year in 2024–2025. No transactions fall on Saturday or Sunday, so both bars are zero, while Monday (20,126) is roughly 1.5 times any other day. Bars on a common baseline make these counts easy to compare, and the zero labels show the empty weekend days explicitly.

Task 2: Monthly sales, off-plan vs existing properties

Which month had the most off-plan sales? Off-plan sales were highest in September 2025, with 706 sales (existing properties had 316 that month). The next highest months were October 2024 (665) and December 2025 (656). Off-plan sales exceeded existing sales in every month of 2024 and 2025, while April–May 2020 shows the COVID-19 dip for both.

Task 3: Median price per m² of units and villas by community

Which community has the most sales of units and villas? Al Barsha South Fourth (community 681, around Jumeirah Village Circle) has the most unit and villa sales: 3,925, with a median of about 12,900 AED/m². Business Bay (3,634) and Marsa Dubai (2,955) follow. The map is not effective for finding this community: colour encodes the median price, not the number of sales, so the only way to find it is to hover over every community, and as a mid-priced community it does not stand out. A bar chart of sales per community, sorted from highest to lowest, would show the answer at a glance. On the other hand, the map does its intended job well: communities with fewer than 10 sales are grey so unreliable medians are not coloured, and price classes with round breaks keep the skewed prices (most 10,000–25,000 AED/m², a few up to 75,000) spread across the colour range.
